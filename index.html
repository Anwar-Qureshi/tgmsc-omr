OMR Evaluator

Grade mock-test answer sheets and verify each result.

Set up an exam

Choose the form, set the marking rules, and confirm the answer key.

Choose the number of questions, not the total marks. Enter the marking rules for this particular exam; the form does not determine the deduction.

Enter exactly 100 answers: A, B, C or D in question order. Spaces, commas and line breaks are allowed. Do not include question numbers. Image answer keys are not supported; type or paste the verified key.

Check the answer key

Compare these entries with the exam’s answer key before grading.

Add an answer sheet

On a computer, upload an existing photo. To take a new photo, open the evaluator on your phone.

Keep the sheet flat and upright, with all four printed corner marks visible. Hold the phone nearly parallel to the paper, fill most of the photo with the sheet, and avoid blur, glare and shadows. Photograph it before adding correction marks.

The photo is read automatically for supported forms. If the camera does not open, take a photo in your phone’s Camera app and use Upload photo.

Review answers

Choose what the student marked, not the correct answer from the key. Use All questions to check every reading. A flagged response needs your decision; Blank means no answer. Use Invalid only if your exam rules treat that response as wrong.

Confirmed result

Copy these figures onto the paper before choosing Next student. This app does not keep a student result history. Marks are displayed to two decimal places.

Quick help & privacy
  1. Set up the exam and check the complete answer key.
  2. Photograph one sheet. Check that the green circles match the printed bubbles.
  3. If alignment is wrong, retake the photo. Manual alignment uses the four printed corner marks, not the answer bubbles.
  4. Review readings, confirm the result, and copy it to the paper before moving on.

The 100-question reader uses the supplied reference. The 50-question reader uses a reconstructed reference and needs real-paper validation. The 200-question reader supports the 5-option grand test form. The 30-question form requires manual response entry. Unusual angles, rotated pages and damaged sheets may require a new photo.

Photos are processed in this browser and are not uploaded by the evaluator. Closing or reloading the tab loses the current sheet and score. Only the exam name, answer key and marking rules are saved locally. Back to exam setup lets you change them.

Photos and scores stay in this tab. Only exam settings are saved on this device.
