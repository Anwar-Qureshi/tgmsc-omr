OMR Evaluator

Check a student’s OMR sheet, review the answers and calculate the score.

Set up an exam

Choose the question count, enter the marks and check your answer key.

Use this exam’s marking rules. Enter the deduction as a positive number, such as 0.5 or 2/3. Enter 0 if there is no negative marking.

Type or paste exactly 100 answers using A, B, C or D. Separate them with spaces, commas or new lines. Leave out question numbers. Answer-key photos cannot be read.

Check the answer key

Check each question number and answer against the official key. If anything is wrong, change the answers above and check again.

Add a student’s answer sheet

Using a computer? Choose a saved photo. To take a photo, open this website on your phone.

Lay the sheet flat and keep its heading at the top of the photo. Include the whole sheet and all four printed corner marks. Take a clear photo in even light, before adding correction marks.

For supported 50- and 100-question sheets, reading starts after you choose a photo.

Review answers

For each question, select the answer the student marked. Select No answer if it is blank. Use All questions to check every answer before confirming.

Confirmed result

Write these totals on the paper before choosing Next student. The previous result will then be cleared. Scores are shown to two decimal places.

Help & privacy

Camera not opening? Take a photo with your phone’s Camera app, then choose Upload photo.

Green circles in the wrong place? Retake the photo with the phone directly above the flat sheet. If needed, choose Set corner marks and tap the four small printed marks around the form. Do not tap the answer bubbles.

More than one option marked? Check the exam’s rules. Choose Invalid / count as wrong only when those rules require a deduction.

Which sheets work? Photo reading supports the supplied TGMSC 100-question layout. The 50-question layout was recreated from a marked sheet and still needs checking on fresh papers. For 30 questions, enter every student answer yourself. Other layouts may not match, even if the question count is the same. Always review the answers.

What is saved? The evaluator reads photos on this device. It does not upload them or keep a student record. Reloading or closing this tab clears the exam, photo and result. Edit current exam keeps the key for editing; Home / New exam clears it after you confirm.

Copy each result before moving on. Reloading or closing this tab clears your work.
